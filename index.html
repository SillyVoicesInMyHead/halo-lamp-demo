Four things it does, and nothing else.

The sunrise curve

Thirty minutes from 1800 K to 5200 K, on a curve shaped like a real horizon, slow at first and then all at once. Set the length anywhere from 10 to 60 minutes.

Dusk, in reverse

At night the same light sets. It falls from warm amber to dark over twenty minutes, so reading in bed ends in sleep instead of glare.

One dial, no app

Turn the brass dial for brightness. Press to snooze, hold to stop. Your phone can sleep in another room.

A backup you won't need

If you haven't stirred ten minutes after full light, a soft chime fades in. It is the only sound Halo ever makes.
